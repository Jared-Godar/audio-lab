# Session Two: The Results Are In

Last session ended with homework: take the Young Schema Questionnaire. This session, the host's real answer sheet — all 232 items, pen on paper, margin notes included — gets read out loud by Dr. Anna Sinclair, the show's synthetic clinician (she discloses this herself; it's kind of her thing). Three schemas light up: Emotional Deprivation, loudest by a mile; an "Insufficient Self-Control" score whose fine print is a textbook ADHD fingerprint; and Self-Sacrifice, the one that rings truest and measures worst. The evidence behind each gets graded live — including the honest headline that the entire adult schema-ADHD literature is two small studies that disagree — and the episode closes with a three-experiment homework plan and a cliffhanger involving an actual, licensed, real human doctor.
