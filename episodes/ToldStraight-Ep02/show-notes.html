Session one. You got the adult ADHD diagnosis, the pills quiet the noise - but they don't touch the 40 years of conclusions you drew while nobody knew. This is the skills half of 'pills plus skills': a first-session tour of CBT, ACT, and the gloriously overloaded label 'MCT' - plus Young's schemas, the negative core beliefs underneath. Told straight: what's evidenced, what isn't, and what to do about beliefs built on a false premise. Homework at the end.
