Told Straight ("we," "us") runs `toldstraight.com`. This policy explains the one piece of
personal data the site handles: an email address you choose to give us so we can tell you
when the show launches.

## What we collect

- **Your email address**, and only if you type it into the "Notify me" form and submit it.
  Nothing else is required and nothing else is asked for.

We do **not** run third-party advertising trackers, and the Coming Soon page sets no
analytics cookies.

## Why we collect it, and the legal basis

We use your email address to send you:

1. one message when the show goes live, and
2. occasional updates about new episodes.

The basis is your **consent**, given by submitting the form. You can withdraw it at any
time (see _Unsubscribe_).

## Where it is stored, and who can see it

Your address is stored in our own **Amazon SES contact list**, inside our AWS account. The
list is **not shared, sold, or rented** to anyone. It is processed by Amazon Web Services
as our infrastructure provider; no other third party receives it.

## How long we keep it

Until you unsubscribe, or until you ask us to delete it, or until the list is no longer
used — whichever comes first.

## Unsubscribe and your choices

- **Unsubscribe** from any email using the link it contains, or by emailing
  **<hello@toldstraight.com>**.
- You may ask us to **show you** the data we hold about you, or to **delete** it, by
  emailing **<hello@toldstraight.com>**. We will act on the request promptly.

## Children

The show is for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with an updated effective date.

Questions about your data: **<hello@toldstraight.com>**.
