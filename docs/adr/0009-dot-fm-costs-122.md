# ADR 0009 — `.fm` costs $122/yr

- **Number:** 0009
- **Title:** `.fm` costs $122/yr
- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** Not recorded in the source entry.
- **Source:** `ROADMAP.md` § "Decisions and what they constrain" (entry migrated by #62).

## Context

Route 53 does sell `.fm` — the earlier uncertainty is resolved. The price is $122/yr.

## Decision

`toldstraight.fm` was skipped as a $610/5yr redirect. The print/journalism property
should be a `.com`, which also fits it better.

## Consequences

**Constrains M6 and branding:** `vote.toldstraight.com` is a subdomain, costing
nothing.

## Reversal condition

Not recorded at decision time.
