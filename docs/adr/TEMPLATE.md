# ADR NNNN — Title

- **Number:** NNNN
- **Title:** a specific declarative sentence, not a bare verb phrase
- **Status:** `accepted` | `superseded by NNNN` | `reversed`
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD — the date the decision was made. If migrating a decision whose
  date was never recorded, write `Not recorded in the source entry.` — do not infer one.
- **Source:** the issue, PR, or file the decision was made in, by number or path.

## Context

What was true, and what forced a choice. Measured facts belong here with their receipts;
an assertion without a command behind it does not.

## Decision

What was decided, stated plainly. Where the deciding text exists (an issue comment, a
maintainer statement, a CHANGELOG entry), **quote it verbatim** — paraphrase belongs in
the surrounding commentary, never in place of the source. A decision restated more
elegantly is a decision quietly altered.

## Consequences

What this decision constrains going forward. Name the milestone(s) it binds — the
`**Constrains M<n>:**` linkage is the most load-bearing part of a decision record.

## Reversal condition

**Required, not optional.** Every recommendation names what would have to be true to
reverse it. Where the source recorded no reversal condition, write exactly
`Not recorded at decision time.` — do **not** invent one after the fact.
